# TIC 308184057: possible NEW hot-giant transit candidate (faint-star TGLC search)

**Status, 2026-11-06: best signal of the project.** It survives every test available here, and we found it in no list we can reach. Novelty is still unconfirmed (ExoFOP is blocked here), and it is not validated (no high-resolution imaging, no mass).

## Star
TIC 308184057: Tmag 14.50, Teff 4339 K, R★ 0.73 R☉, logg 4.54 (K dwarf), distance 424 pc. RA 130.4107, Dec −68.9675 (near the southern CVZ). Gaia DR3 5223463775709403520.

## Signal
- **Found by** `tglc_search.py` (TGLC sectors 2, 9, 10, 11): P = 6.11899 d, depth 1.6%, T14 ≈ 2 h, SNR 17, SDE 16.
- **`tglc_vet.py`:** out-of-transit fold is flat (χ²/dof 0.68 at P and 0.57 at 2P), and odd/even depths are consistent.
- **Independent TARS photometry, 17 sectors from 2018 to 2025** (`tic308184057_tars.png`):
  - The transit is present in every sector, at 0.9–1.4% depth.
  - Odd 11,923 ± 541 ppm vs even 11,678 ± 513 ppm (0.3σ).
  - Secondary at phase 0.5: −190 ± 423 ppm, i.e. none.
  - **Refined P = 6.119015 d, T0 = BTJD 1356.2511.**
- **Size:** ≈ 0.8–0.9 R_J if the signal is on the target. A 2-h duration implies b ≈ 0.75, which is consistent with the stellar density (the central-transit maximum is ≈ 3 h).

## Pixel test (`diff_tesscut_stack.py`, TESScut FFIs, 16 sectors; log `results/diff_308184057.log`)
- **Combined difference-image centroid:** (−0.02 ± 0.11, −0.20 ± 0.12) px from the target, 1.6σ. Fourteen of 16 sectors are within 2.3σ; two outliers sit at about 4σ.
- **The 22″ neighbour TIC 308184061 (Tmag 15.8) is strongly disfavoured:** χ² = 124 vs 48 for the target over 32 dof (Δχ² = 76).
- Other neighbours at 26–40″ are excluded at 2–6σ in most sectors.
- **Remaining blend space:** sources unresolved by TESS (≲ 5″), i.e. a bound or background EB within a few arcseconds. This needs high-resolution imaging or Gaia DR3 RUWE / ipd_frac_multi_peak.
