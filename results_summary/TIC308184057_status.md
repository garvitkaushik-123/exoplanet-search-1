- **Raw verdict (full data): FPP = 0.19, NFPP = 0.17.** This is "likely planet" (FPP < 0.5) but **not validated** (validation needs FPP < 0.015 and NFPP < 0.001).
  - Planet on target: P(TP + PTP + DTP) = 0.81.
  - Nearly all of the false-positive probability is nearby-star scenarios: NEB 0.13 and NTP 0.04, from TIC 308184058, 308184047, 308184061 and 308184038.

  - What remains are the SEB (0.015) and STP (0.014) scenarios on the target itself.
  - **This is our own analysis, not the standard TRICERATOPS number,** and must be labelled that way.
  - High-resolution imaging (a contrast curve) would cut the SEB/STP space further and could bring the FPP below the 0.015 validation threshold.
