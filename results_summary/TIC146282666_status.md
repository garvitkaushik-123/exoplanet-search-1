## Decisive, cheap test
Ground-based photometry with seeing ≲ 3″ at one predicted transit, measuring TIC 632015734 separately. If the neighbour does not dim by about 7%, it is cleared, and the signal is on the target or on an unresolved companion. Seeing-limited TFOP SG1 observers do this routinely, and a 7% dip on a T = 15.4 star is easy.
